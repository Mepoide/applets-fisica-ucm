# Recommended bibliography

## Statistical Physics

- K. Huang, *Statistical Mechanics*, 2nd ed., Wiley, 1987.
- R. K. Pathria and P. D. Beale, *Statistical Mechanics*, 3rd ed., Elsevier, 2011.
- G. H. Wannier, *Statistical Physics*, Dover, 1987.
- W. Greiner, L. Neise and H. Stöcker, *Thermodynamics and Statistical Mechanics*, Springer, 1995.
- S. E. Koonin and D. C. Meredith, *Computational Physics*, Addison-Wesley, 1990.
- H. Gould and J. Tobochnik, *Statistical and Thermal Physics with Computer Applications*, Princeton, 2010.

## Non-equilibrium Systems

- F. Reif, *Statistical Physics* (Berkeley Physics Course, vol. 5), McGraw-Hill, 1967.
- J. R. Dorfman, *An Introduction to Chaos in Nonequilibrium Statistical Mechanics*, Cambridge, 1999.
- R. Ghez, *A Primer on Diffusion Problems*, Wiley, 1988.
- N. G. van Kampen, *Stochastic Processes in Physics and Chemistry*, 3rd ed., North-Holland, 2007.
- P. Hänggi, P. Talkner and M. Borkovec, "Reaction-rate theory: fifty years after Kramers", *Rev. Mod. Phys.* 62, 251 (1990).
- L. Gammaitoni, P. Hänggi, P. Jung and F. Marchesoni, "Stochastic resonance", *Rev. Mod. Phys.* 70, 223 (1998).
- U. Frisch, B. Hasslacher and Y. Pomeau, "Lattice-gas automata for the Navier–Stokes equation", *Phys. Rev. Lett.* 56, 1505 (1986); J.-P. Rivet and J. P. Boon, *Lattice Gas Hydrodynamics*, Cambridge, 2001.
- P. Grosfils, J. P. Boon, R. Brito and M. H. Ernst, "Statistical hydrodynamics of lattice-gas automata", *Phys. Rev. E* 48, 2655 (1993).
- P. Bak, *How Nature Works*, Copernicus, 1996; K. Christensen and N. R. Moloney, *Complexity and Criticality*, Imperial College Press, 2005.

## Solid State Physics

- N. W. Ashcroft and N. D. Mermin, *Solid State Physics*, Saunders, 1976.
- C. Kittel, *Introduction to Solid State Physics*, Wiley, 8th ed., 2005.
- F. Domínguez-Adame, *Física del estado sólido. Teoría y métodos numéricos*, Paraninfo, 2000.
- O. Madelung, *Introduction to Solid State Theory*, Springer, 1988.
- E. Maciá and F. Domínguez-Adame, *Electrons, Phonons and Excitons in Low-dimensional Aperiodic Systems*, Editorial Complutense, 2000.
- K. Seeger, *Semiconductor Physics*, Springer, 1991.

## Quantum Devices

- J. H. Davies, *The Physics of Low-Dimensional Semiconductors*, Cambridge, 1998.
- Y. Fu and M. Willander, *Physical Models of Semiconductor Quantum Devices*, Kluwer, 1999.
- S. L. Chuang, *Physics of Optoelectronic Devices*, Wiley, 1995.
- F. T. Vasko and A. V. Kuznetsov, *Electronic States and Optical Transitions in Semiconductor Heterostructures*, Springer, 1999.

## About the original course
