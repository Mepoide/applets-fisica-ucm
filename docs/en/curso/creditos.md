# Credits

## Original course (UCM, 2002)

*Experimentos Interactivos en Sistemas Físicos Complejos* was coordinated by **Ricardo Brito López**, professor at the Department of Applied Physics I (Thermology), and **Francisco Domínguez-Adame Acosta**, professor at the Department of Materials Physics, Universidad Complutense de Madrid, funded by two UCM Educational Innovation Projects (2000-01 and 2001-02).

- *Statistical Physics* and *Non-equilibrium Systems* contents: Ricardo Brito, with **Juan M. R. Parrondo** (Department of Atomic, Molecular and Nuclear Physics).
- *Solid State Physics* and *Quantum Devices* contents, and the original web design: Francisco Domínguez-Adame.
- Java programming: **Mario Castro Ponce** (Universidad Pontificia Comillas) and **Pedro Hidalgo Alcalde** (UCM).
- Collaboration in the Statistical Physics practicals: **Mario Encinar**, who developed in 2003, as a supervised project (TAD) with R. Brito, the lattice-gas (sound wave, Von Kármán), hard-disk and Maxwell, sandpile and hydrodynamic-modes applets.
- The original Brownian-motion applet was by Michael Fowler (University of Virginia).

Original credits page: <http://valbuena.fis.ucm.es/expint/html/desarrollado.html>.

## This version (2026)

- JavaScript rewrite of the 21 experiments published so far, physics audit, sheet texts, tests and deployment: **Mario Encinar**, assisted by Claude (Anthropic).
- The sheets are new texts; the original course is cited as the source of each experiment and its texts are not reproduced.
- Open source at <https://github.com/Mepoide/applets-fisica-ucm>. The 2002-2003 Java code is preserved unchanged on the `legacy-java` branch.
