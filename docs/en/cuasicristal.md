# Atomic vibrations of a quasicrystal

## What you see

The same harmonic chain as in the previous experiment, but with the two masses ordered by the **Fibonacci sequence** ABAABABA…, neither periodic nor random: the simplest one-dimensional model of a quasicrystal. The integrated density of modes shows a hierarchy of plateaus (gaps) that splits as the generation grows: the spectrum is a Cantor set.

## Physical model

The sequence is generated by the substitution rule A → B, B → AB (equivalently $m_n = M$ when $\lfloor (n+2)/\tau\rfloor - \lfloor (n+1)/\tau\rfloor = 0$, with $\tau = (1+\sqrt5)/2$); after $n$ steps it has $F_n$ elements, with $F_n = F_{n-1} + F_{n-2}$, and the fraction of heavy masses tends to $1/\tau^2 \approx 0.382$. The equation of motion is that of experiment 2 and is solved likewise by node counting. Unlike the random case, here eigenstates are **critical** (neither extended nor exponentially localised) and the spectrum is singular continuous: at every generation new gaps open inside the bands of the previous one, in a self-similar structure governed by $\tau$.

## Algorithm

`fibonacciMasses(F_n, α)` and `integratedDOS` from `applets/_chain/chain.js`; generations $8 \le n \le 17$ ($F_{17} = 2584$ atoms).
